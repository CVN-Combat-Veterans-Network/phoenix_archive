# Triadic Atlas — Page I  
## The Three Pillars of the Codex Cosmological Model

### Hydrogenesi — Pillar of Origin
Symbol: Η →  
Domain: Grounding, Coherence, Nucleus  
Function: Establishes the origin state from which all lineage emerges.

### Phoenix — Pillar of Projection
Symbol: Φ  
Domain: Linguistic Projection, Semantic Structure  
Function: Gives form its voice, meaning, and narrative trajectory.

### The Third — Pillar of Apex
Symbol: Δ³  
Domain: Triadic Integration, Apex Authority  
Function: Binds all lineage into apex convergence and governs expansion.
